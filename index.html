ABOUT & RESPONSIBLE USE

What this tool is, what it is not, and how to read its output.

What MineStackerBot is

MineStackerBot is an estimated Mines analysis and visualization interface. You provide a client seed and a mine count; the app derives a deterministic 5 × 5 grid visualization from those inputs and highlights a small number of estimated safe tiles.

It is not a playable Mines game. There is no cell interaction: all 25 cells are generated and displayed automatically, and the tool never opens, selects or reveals cells on your behalf.

Responsible use — please read

  • Generated visualizations are estimates and simulations. They do not predict, promise or influence future game outcomes.
  • The “estimated confidence” figure shown after an analysis is a simulated, illustrative metric derived from your seed. It is not a measured accuracy, hit rate or win probability, and it should not be used as a reason to place any bet.
  • This tool cannot determine the real mine layout of a provably-fair game. True mine positions are never calculated and never displayed anywhere in the app.
  • Gambling carries real financial risk. Only ever risk money you can afford to lose, set your own limits, and stop if it stops being entertainment.
  • 18+ only, and only where participation is legal for you.
  • If gambling is causing you harm, seek support from a local problem-gambling service.

How the visualization is produced

Your seed string and the selected mine count are hashed (xmur3) into a deterministic random stream (mulberry32). That stream selects how many safe tiles are revealed — using a fixed table keyed on your mine count — and shuffles the 25 cell positions to decide which cells they land on.

The same seed with the same mine count always renders the same grid; changing either input changes the grid. All remaining cells are simply left unrevealed and carry no information whatsoever.

Privacy & offline operation

Everything runs locally in your browser. There are no network requests, no analytics, no external fonts or scripts, and no seed ever leaves your device. Your settings, profile and analysis history are stored in your browser's localStorage and can be cleared at any time from the History page or by clearing site data.

Watch the flow

No video file ships with this offline build — here is the exact sequence the demo runs:

  1. Paste a seed. Any client seed or seed hash. The field accepts the format you already use.
  2. Select mines (1–24). Counts of 10 and above are flagged ⚠ HIGH RISK.
  3. Press ANALYZE GRID. The analysis sequence runs for one to three seconds.
  4. Read the grid. Estimated safe tiles appear as gems; every other cell stays unrevealed.
